FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? I've got answers. Here are some of the most common things people ask before we start working together.

What is your design process?

My design process follows a structured yet flexible approach. It typically begins with Discovery & Research — understanding your business goals, users, and competitive landscape. I then move to Ideation, exploring concepts through wireframes and sketches. Next comes Design, where I craft high-fidelity UI with attention to every pixel. I prototype key interactions for Testing, gather feedback, and iterate. Finally, I ensure a smooth Handoff with developers, providing specs, assets, and ongoing support.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A landing page or simple UI may take 1-2 weeks. A full mobile app or web application typically ranges from 4-8 weeks. Larger product design or design system projects can span 2-3 months or more. I always provide a detailed timeline during the discovery phase so there are no surprises.

What are your rates?

I offer flexible pricing models depending on the project type. For smaller engagements, I work on a fixed project fee. For ongoing collaborations, I offer monthly retainers. I also provide hourly consulting for UX audits or advisory sessions. Every project begins with a free discovery call where we scope the work together, and I provide a transparent quote before any commitment.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely! I've had the pleasure of working with clients across the US, UK, Europe, Middle East, and Southeast Asia. Remote collaboration is second nature to me. I'm comfortable working across time zones and use tools like Figma, Slack, Zoom, and Notion to keep communication seamless. Whether you're in New York or Singapore, we can create something great together.

What tools do you use?

My primary tools include Figma for UI design, prototyping, and design systems. For research and planning, I use Miro and Notion. I create high-fidelity prototypes in Framer and use Principle for advanced micro-interactions. For visual design and assets, I use Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop. I'm always exploring new tools and happy to adapt to your team's stack.
