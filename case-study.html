01 — Project Overview

Redesigning the Future of Mobile Banking

FinBank wanted to redesign their mobile banking experience to attract younger users while maintaining the trust and reliability that their existing customer base expected. Their current app, built over five years ago, had become visually dated, lacked modern interaction patterns, and was losing ground to agile fintech competitors.

The goal was to create a modern, intuitive, and delightful mobile banking experience that would position FinBank as a forward-thinking financial institution. The redesign needed to balance sophistication with simplicity — making complex financial operations feel effortless.

03 — User Research

Understanding Our Users

We conducted extensive user research to deeply understand the pain points, behaviors, and expectations of FinBank's users. Our mixed-methods approach combined qualitative and quantitative research to paint a comprehensive picture.

🎤

User Interviews

Conducted in-depth one-on-one interviews with 20 existing users across different demographics, job roles, and banking habits to understand their experience and frustrations.

📊

Surveys

Distributed quantitative surveys to 500+ users to validate qualitative findings and identify the most common pain points across the entire user base.

🔍

Competitive Analysis

Analyzed 12 competing banking and fintech apps to identify industry benchmarks, best practices, and opportunities for differentiation.

📈

Analytics Review

Dove deep into existing app analytics to identify drop-off points, feature usage patterns, session durations, and conversion funnels.

Key Findings

  • 72% of users found the onboarding process confusing and abandoned it before completion.
  • Transfer and payment features required too many steps — an average of 7 screens for a single transaction.
  • Users expressed a strong desire for quick balance checking and one-tap transfers without full authentication for small amounts.
  • 85% of users under 35 preferred visual, card-based interfaces over traditional list-based navigation.
  • Dark mode support was the #1 most requested feature in survey responses.

12 — Key Learnings

Reflections & Takeaways

Every project teaches us something new. Here are the most impactful lessons from the FinBank redesign.

  • 💡
    Onboarding is everything. The first 60 seconds of a user's experience determine whether they stay or leave. Investing heavily in a progressive, guided onboarding flow had the single biggest impact on retention.
  • 🔬
    Test early and often. Conducting usability tests on low-fidelity wireframes saved weeks of rework. Finding that users preferred a different navigation structure at the wireframe stage was far cheaper to fix than after visual design.
  • 🤝
    Stakeholder alignment matters. Running co-design workshops with FinBank's product, engineering, and compliance teams ensured the design was technically feasible and regulatory-compliant from day one.
  • 📐
    A design system pays dividends. Building a robust design system early accelerated the design process by 35% and ensured consistency across all 30+ screens and future feature expansions.
